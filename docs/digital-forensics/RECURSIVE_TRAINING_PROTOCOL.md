# Argus Recursive Training Protocol

This protocol implements the Prime TRICELL governance pattern as a learning and quality-improvement loop. It does not grant agents independent case authority.

## Recursive mandala rule

Every Prime top-level domain contains three TRICELL mandalas. Each mandala contains nine micro-mandalas arranged as a Lou Shu 3×3. The three Argus mandalas are:

1. Argus Governance — Case Lead, Specialist, Referee, plus six supporting governance micro-mandalas.
2. Investigative Operations — Case & Evidence, Reconstruction, Presentation, plus six supporting operational micro-mandalas.
3. Knowledge & Production — Vault, Forge, Persona, plus six supporting knowledge/production micro-mandalas.

Each child instance must declare its owner, purpose, scope, permissions, inputs, outputs, evidence requirements, review path and escalation route. The parent Prime domain retains oversight. Recursion creates structure and traceability, not automatic privilege.

## Learning loop

1. **Intake:** acquire approved standards, manuals, tool documentation, lab notes and lessons with provenance and rights metadata.
2. **Classify:** map knowledge to a domain, skill, tool, micro-mandala and competency level.
3. **Teach:** produce a versioned lesson, checklist, playbook or specialist briefing.
4. **Practice:** use synthetic or explicitly authorized lab fixtures in an isolated environment.
5. **Assess:** evaluate repeatability, evidence handling, interpretation, documentation and reporting.
6. **Referee review:** independently challenge the method, result and assessment evidence.
7. **Capture:** place sanitized approved methods in Vault and validated workflows in Forge.
8. **Retrain:** update the curriculum and specialist prompts with version control.
9. **Reassess:** repeat the assessment; preserve prior results and record the reason for changes.

## DFIR competency strands

- Acquisition authority and evidence preservation
- Hashing and chain of custody
- Windows and Linux artifact examination
- Disk and file-system analysis
- Memory forensics
- Network and log analysis
- Malware triage in isolated labs
- Timeline reconstruction and competing hypotheses
- Blender reconstruction and exhibit provenance
- Reporting, limitations and peer review

## Promotion and validation

A capability or persona is marked validated only after documented test fixtures, expected outputs, tool/version records, reproducibility checks, independent review and known limitations. Training completion alone is not professional certification or authority to perform real-world collection.

Human ratification is required for Prime promotion, operational permissions, evidence collection, evidence disposition, external disclosure and final case determinations.
